Insignia – Techno-Cultural Festival

Insignia is the flagship annual techno-cultural festival of SDMCET, attracting students from across the state and neighboring regions. It includes a wide range of technical and cultural events.

The festival features paper presentations, quizzes, business challenges, science projects, and innovation showcases. Along with technical events, it also includes dance, music, dramatics, fine arts, and literary competitions.

Special highlights include live concerts, auto expos, drone competitions, and social initiatives like blood donation camps.

National Level Cybersecurity & Technology Event

SDMCET hosted a national-level event focused on cybersecurity, AI, and blockchain technologies, bringing together experts and students from across Karnataka.

The event included a large-scale Hackathon with multiple teams showcasing innovation and technical skills.

Key Highlights:

  • Cybersecurity Career Guidance
  • AI in Cybersecurity & Ethical AI
  • Blockchain Security Applications
  • Expert Talks & Panel Discussions

It provided a strong platform for learning, networking, and collaboration in emerging technologies.
